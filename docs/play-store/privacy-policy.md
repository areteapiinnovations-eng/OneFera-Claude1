OneFera ("we", "us") is operated by **[legal entity name, registered address]**. This policy explains what personal data the OneFera app collects, why, and the choices you have.

## 1. What we collect

| What | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Name, username, email, password (stored hashed by Firebase Authentication) | When you sign up | To create and secure your account |
| Date of birth | When you sign up | To confirm you're old enough and to apply under-18 safety settings |
| Profile details (photo, bio, city, vibe) | If you add them | To show your profile |
| Posts, reels, stories, comments, messages, attachments | When you share them | To provide the app's features |
| Follows, likes, saves | As you use the app | To build your feed and Aura score |
| Delivery name, phone and address | When you place an order | To deliver orders; shared with the seller(s) in the order |
| Order and purchase history | When you buy or sell | For orders, refunds, tax and seller analytics |
| Approximate location (~1 km) | Only if you use Near | To show nearby people and stores; only stored while you choose to be visible or list a store |
| Device push token | If notifications are on | To send notifications |
| Voice messages | When you record one in a chat | To deliver it to the person you're chatting with |
| Seller details: legal name, PAN, GSTIN, business address, phone, UPI ID or bank account | When you register as a seller | To verify sellers, prevent fraud, pay out sales and meet tax rules. Never shown publicly; only your store name is |
| Story views and mentions | When you view or mention someone in a story | To show a story's author who has seen it, and to notify the people you mention |

We **do not** collect your precise location or contacts, and we never collect buyers' card or UPI details. Payments are handled by **Razorpay** and subscriptions by **Google Play**, under their own privacy policies.

## 2. How we use data

We use data to provide and secure the service, deliver orders, show relevant content, calculate Aura, streaks and rewards, prevent fraud and abuse, and communicate with you about your account. We don't sell personal data and don't use third-party advertising SDKs.

## 3. Who we share with

- **Other users:** what you post publicly, your profile, and (if you opt in) your approximate distance on Near.
- **Sellers:** the delivery details needed for orders you place with them.
- **Service providers:** Google Firebase (hosting, database, authentication, storage, notifications), Razorpay (payments) and Google Play (subscriptions). They process data on our behalf.
- **Legal:** when required by law or to protect users' safety.

You can delete your account at any time in **Settings → Delete account** or at https://onefera.app/delete-account. This deletes your profile, posts, stories, listings, cart, rewards, Near entries and uploaded files.
