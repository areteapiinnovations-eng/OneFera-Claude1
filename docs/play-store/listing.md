## Full description (≤ 4000 characters)

> **OneFera is One Future Era: the social app where your feed is also your shop.**
>
> Post moments and reels, chat with your circle, discover drops from creators you love, and open your own store. All of it in one app, built for Gen Z.
>
> **✨ Share your world**
> • Photo posts, full-screen reels and 24-hour stories
> • Hashtags, trending tags and a For You + Following feed
> • Likes, comments, saves and follow requests for private accounts
>
> **💬 Chat that keeps up**
> • Real-time DMs with replies, photos and files
> • Typing indicators, read receipts and "Active now"
> • Quick vibes for when words aren't enough
>
> **🛍️ Shop without leaving the feed**
> • Tap-to-buy product tags on posts and reels
> • Drop of the Day, trending picks, categories, filters and sort
> • Wishlist, cart and secure checkout with UPI, cards, net banking or cash on delivery
> • Live order tracking from packed to delivered
>
> **🏪 Sell your stuff**
> • Switch to a Seller account in one tap
> • List products with photos, sizes, stock and prices
> • Fulfil orders, track sales and see your best sellers in the Seller hub
>
> **⚡ Level up your Aura**
> • Earn Aura for posting and for the love you get back
> • Keep your daily 🔥 streak alive and open a Mystery Box every day
> • Win coupons, climb the leaderboard and flex your grade
>
> **📍 Near**
> • Discover creators and stores around you, privacy first
> • Approximate distance only. Off by default. Under-18s are never shown
>
> **💎 OneFera+ and Seller Pro**
> • More Mystery Boxes, double streak Aura, free delivery
> • Unlimited listings and 30-day sales analytics for sellers
>
> Make it yours with 5 theme skins: Future Era, Cyber Neon, Sunset Pop, Matrix Lime and Aurora Ice, in dark or light.
>
> OneFera is for ages 13+. Some features (Near people) are 18+.
